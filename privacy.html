Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Who we are

LittleLeap News is a news show for children made by LittleLeap Studios and broadcast on the LittleLeap Studios YouTube channel. Contact: uxtestsmkda@gmail.com.

Viewers

LittleLeap News does not collect any personal information from viewers. Our videos are marked "made for kids" on YouTube. Watching them is governed by Google's privacy policy and YouTube's terms.

Our Google app

The LittleLeap News app asks for access to a YouTube account only so that LittleLeap Studios can manage its own channel: schedule live editions, give them titles, descriptions and thumbnails, add them to playlists, and read the channel's own view statistics. It is used only with the LittleLeap Studios channel.

  • The access permission is stored on our own server and is used for nothing else.
  • We do not read, store or share information about other people's accounts.
  • We do not sell or share any data, and we show no advertising based on it.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date.
