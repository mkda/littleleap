LittleLeap News

Happy news for kids aged 3 to 7, live on YouTube from the LittleLeap Studios channel. Zippy, Tomo and their friends share good news from around the world, fun facts, new words, counting, kindness and the local weather.

Editions go live every morning and after school for friends in the UK and Ireland, the US East Coast and Eastern Australia.

Watch LittleLeap Studios on YouTube

The LittleLeap News app on Google is only used by LittleLeap Studios itself, to schedule its own live editions on its own YouTube channel and read its own channel statistics.
