What LittleLeap is

LittleLeap Studios makes gentle cartoon stories for children aged 3 to 7. We retell classic public-domain fables and fairy tales, such as Aesop and the Brothers Grimm, in our own words, with our own original characters: Zippy the hare, Tomo the tortoise, Silvia the squirrel, Little Sugar the bear and their friends from the Big Meadow.

  • No villains and no scary moments: problems are solved with kindness and teamwork.
  • Calm endings, so a story works well before a nap or bedtime.
  • Join-in moments: counting, waving, flapping like Zippy.
  • About 10 minutes per story, with chapters. A new one every two weeks.

How we use AI

The stories are written, directed and checked by a person. We use AI tools to help draw the pictures, voice the characters and compose the music, and every episode is reviewed by hand before it goes online. Each YouTube description says that AI tools were used.

Safety and privacy

  • All our videos are marked “Made for Kids” on YouTube.
  • This website has no ads, no cookies, no trackers and no sign-up. Videos open on YouTube.
